### Don't just predict the harvest. Simulate the season before it happens.

AI What-If Season Planner is a farmer-focused agricultural intelligence platform that goes beyond traditional crop-yield prediction.

Instead of giving a farmer a single predicted number, the platform allows them to explore different seasonal conditions, understand uncertainty, examine historical agricultural data, understand factors associated with a model estimate, and discover potentially relevant government schemes.

---

## 🚀 What Problem Are We Solving?

Agricultural yield depends on many changing conditions, but conventional prediction systems often present farmers with a single estimated yield without clearly communicating:

- How much historical data supports the estimate
- How uncertain the estimate is
- What could happen under different rainfall conditions
- How similar historical seasons performed
- Which factors are associated with the model prediction
- Which government schemes may potentially be relevant

Farmers also should not be expected to know technical ML inputs or accurately guess future rainfall.

Our goal is therefore not to make the farming decision for the farmer.

Our goal is to make the uncertainty visible, the scenarios explorable, and the information easier to understand.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

The **AI What-If Season Planner** combines:

> **Machine Learning + Historical Agricultural Data + What-If Simulation + Explainability + Uncertainty + Government Scheme Discovery**

The system allows a farmer to:

1. Select their agricultural profile.
2. Generate a machine-learning yield estimate.
3. See an empirical prediction range.
4. Understand the amount of historical data supporting the estimate.
5. Simulate extreme-dry, dry, typical and wet rainfall scenarios.
6. Compare scenarios using the trained model.
7. Explore real historical agricultural records.
8. Understand factors associated with the prediction.
9. Discover potentially relevant government schemes.
10. Explore additional historical analogs and crop comparisons.
